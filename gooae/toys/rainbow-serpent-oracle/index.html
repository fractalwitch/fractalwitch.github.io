Rainbow Serpent Oracle

A long foil serpent of living colour with a prophecy written down its back. Drag and it follows your finger. Poke it and colour waves run along its body. Hold it and it sheds a skin. Feed it a ticket and watch the lump travel all the way to its tail. Leave it alone and it chases its own tail until it bites it, and the loop becomes a door into forever.

    SCALES · HOLO-FOIL

    Living colour

    Every scale is a domed thin-film tile with its own thickness, so the whole body shimmers through the rainbow. The colour bands slowly flow along it.

    MOTION · RIPPLE-BLOOM

    Colour waves

    A poke sends a bright ring travelling both ways along the body. The body swells as the ring passes, and the body itself is a wave, slithering.

    SPINE · GLYPH-FIELD

    A prophecy down its back

    A stripe of letters runs along its spine, scrolling from head to tail. When it eats a prophecy, the prophecy becomes the words on its back.

    LOOP · MIRROR-VAULT

    Ouroboros door

    When it bites its own tail, the hole in the ring opens onto an infinite mirror tunnel. The last thing it ate echoes down the tunnel.

    SHED · TICKET-PRESS

    Shed skins

    Squeeze it and its scales flare. Let go and a ghost of its old skin peels away, dropping a printed prophecy. Swallowed tickets travel down its body as a lump.

    WORLD · GOOAE

    The Voidbloom

    The void where the First Melt happened: a pulsing field of luminous goo, nebulae and the faint first rainbow. Four forms: Rainbow, Voidbloom, Slime and Carnival.
