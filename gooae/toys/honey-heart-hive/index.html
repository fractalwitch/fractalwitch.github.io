HONEY HEART HIVE

A liveable honey world. The honeycomb is a ripple-bloom graph and the glass heart is an oracle creature. Its gut is a mirror vault, its skin is honey foil, and its scars are letters.

MOOD: CURIOUS

tap comb · wave   drag · trace   tap heart · poke   hold heart · squeeze/spit   bubble → heart · feed   tap bubble · pop   flick drips

Species

Heart (oracle creature)

Comb (ripple bloom)

Anatomy

  • Comb: every hex cell is a node with six neighbours. Waves hop from cell to cell and each cell rings like a spring. Cells lift, shift hue, glow cyan, and fill with honey.
  • Heart body: a raymarched SDF heart that beats, squashes and jiggles, with glass that refracts the comb behind it.
  • Nerves: pokes travel across the heart's surface, and each one also kicks a wave out into the comb.
  • Skin: thin-film honey foil over marbled gold and magenta swirl, stirred by the waves.
  • Scars: churning glyphs trail each wave. Their alphabet grows with everything the heart eats.
  • Eye and gut: an almond window looks into an infinite mirror corridor, tinted by the last meal.
  • Voice: squeeze and release to spit a glass heart bubble that carries a glyph.
  • World: drips grow, fall and splash into the pool, which sends waves through the comb or pokes the heart.
