FRACTAL FLUTTER

Slurpy Moth flies only in loops: every path is a chain of tangent semicircles, and its wake draws the circle ladder at every scale. Snake meets Frogger, hosted by the Exposure Garden: the flowers, the fog and the glow are all the garden's.

Swipe to turn (turns happen where the loop allows). Hold to tighten into tiny careful loops. Eat glowing nectar to grow your goo tail. Deep-coloured flowers are ripe and sting; pale ones are safe. Fog rivers carry you. Deliver your tail to an empty mother bloom at the top. Close a loop with your tail to lasso what is inside.
