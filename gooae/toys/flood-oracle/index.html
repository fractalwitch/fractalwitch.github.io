Flood Oracle

The Goo Oracle Egg breathes The Flood. Its eye is a well: when it inhales, sparks and drifting words pour into its gut; when it exhales, the swarm sprays out around it and leaves it in the eye of the storm. Your finger is a second well, so the two of you compete for the flood.

Anatomy

  • Breath (tide-swarm well). The eye's screen position is a well on a breath clock: inhale (sink) → hold → exhale (sideways orbit that sprays outward) → rest. Moods retune it.
  • Lungs (the flood). Up to 40,000 sparks ride The Flood's fixed lattice θ = 2π(sin kx + cos ky), live 50–150 steps and are lit by their speed. The lattice is baked into a lookup grid, so a step costs one square root per well.
  • Depth. Half the sparks swim in front of the egg and half behind, drawn into two fading films. The back film shows only where the ray misses the body; the front film is laid over it. During an inhale, sparks near the eye move to the front so you see them go in.
  • Skin and nerves (holo-foil + ripple-bloom). Front sparks that hit the shell bounce off and add up to pokes, so the flood keeps writing rings and letter scars, and the foil sizzles with the strike rate.
  • Gut (mirror-vault). Swallowed sparks become gulps: the corridor's travelling pulses speed up and flash, and the pupil glows white.
  • Words (The Flood's voice). Phrases from The Flood and from Ælara's transmission drift as heavy particles. Inhaled phrases become its next prophecy. Feed a ticket back and its phrases rejoin the flood, its letters join the skin and its inks recolour the swarm.
  • Rivalry. Hold many sparks at your finger while it inhales and it gets ROBBED: it glares at you, breathes harder and takes them back when you let go.
