ANATOMY

One conserved quantity. 100 units of charge live in five stores (fog, bolt, scar, tissue, foil). Every rule moves charge from one store to the next; nothing mints or loses it. The ledger is summed every frame and float drift is renormalised into the fog.

The fog carries and piles it. A small stable fluid (buoyancy, vorticity confinement, Jacobi projection) moves charge by exactly conservative donor-cell flux, plus a drift relative to the air that piles it against the jar's top (or its glass, in the globe).

Breakdown. When a cell passes the threshold it drains a Gaussian disc and fires a bolt: a stochastic walker toward the ground, the core or a held finger, pulled toward live tissue, with forks. Thunder kicks vortex pairs along the channel.

Light becomes scar. The bolt's charge is light on the exposure; as the film fades (the same decay), the faded share settles along the channel, heaviest at the strike point and branch tips.

Scar quickens tissue. A B/S rule runs on the jar; a birth must pay its cost from the local scar pool, so glass grows only where lightning fell. Tissue leaks charge into foil and dies when starved.

Foil returns it. A thin-film slick whose thickness is the charge it holds evaporates back into the fog.

Hue is lineage. Every share of charge carries a hue vector through every store; each trip through a bolt advances it by spectral × channel length.
