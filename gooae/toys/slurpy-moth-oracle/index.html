Slurpy Moth Oracle

A velvet moth whose wing scales are tiny letters and whose compound eyes each hold an infinite mirror tunnel. Drag a light across the dark and it chases it. Squeeze it and it gives you a little wonder. Offer a prophecy and it uncurls its tongue and slurps it up. Leave a ticket lying around and it will come and steal it.

WONDERS

None yet. Hold the moth until its wings fold shut.

    SCALES · GLYPH-FIELD

    Wings written in letters

    Every wing scale is a glyph, picked by the brightness of the wing pattern under it, so the eyespots, veins and bands are drawn entirely in tiny letters. They churn faster where a ripple passes.

    SHEEN · HOLO-FOIL

    Foil and velvet

    Thin-film foil over the wings, and an iridescent sheen on the fuzzy velvet body. Your lamp lights it, and glows through the wings from behind.

    NERVES · RIPPLE-BLOOM

    Ripples, feelers, goo

    A poke sends a ring across the wing membrane. The feathered antennae light up barb by barb. Goo strands hang from its tails, stretch, and snap off as drops.

    EYES · MIRROR-VAULT

    A thousand tunnels

    Each compound eye is made of hex facets, and every facet is its own small window into an infinite mirror tunnel. A dark pseudopupil follows you. Whatever it slurps shows up in there.

    TONGUE · TICKET-PRESS

    Little wonders

    Squeeze it and it gifts you a printed wonder that flutters to the ground. Offer one back and the curled proboscis unrolls to slurp it. Leave one on the ground and it may come and take it.

    WORLD · GOOAE

    Fractal Gardens at night

    Recursive flower silhouettes, glitter in the soil and the three moons 🌒🌕🌘. The forms are its canon transformations: Slurpy, Gooform, Crystalwing and Fractal Moth.
