ORACLE CREATURE 03 · AMBIENT · PUSH-FOG × LIFE-PULSE

Slurpy Moth · Fog Garden

The Slurpy Moth drifts over a living garden at night. A Life field glows under a layer of ground fog: colonies breathe in time with the moth's heartbeat, and fractal groves bloom into self-similar copies and fold back to a seed. When the moth flies low it wades through the fog and clears a channel; its wingbeats blow the fog outward; every few breaths it exhales coloured fog that swirls and drifts on the wind, and life sprouts where it skims. Drag to lure it, tap the garden to plant, tap the moth to ripple its wings, hold it to make it exhale.

MOOD · DRIFTING
drag · lure   tap garden · plant   tap moth · ripple   hold moth · exhale   wheel/pinch · zoom
GROUND · LIFE-PULSE

A garden that computes

A 192² Life torus tiles the endless floor. Conway colonies breathe between generations, swelling on a heartbeat that ripples outward from the moth. Fractal groves run a parity replicator inside a dead moat and are re-seeded every 8, 16 or 32 generations, so they bloom into copies of copies and fold back.

AIR · PUSH-FOG

Fog with memory

A 2D fluid in the air's own frame holds fog amount, air velocity and a breath dye. One ray-marched pass turns it into a moonlit layer, and the garden's glow lights the fog from below, so colonies show as coloured haze long before you see their cells.

BODY · ORACLE-CREATURE

The moth wades and breathes

Flying low, its body clears the fog and its wingbeats blow it outward; high, it glides above. It exhales tinted fog that the fluid carries, plants seeds where it skims, and plants a small fractal grove under each breath.

SKIN · GLYPH-FIELD + HOLO-FOIL

Wings written in letters

The wing pattern is drawn in glyph scales over thin-film foil. The garden's light shines through the wings from below.

EYES · MIRROR-VAULT

A thousand tunnels

Each hex facet of its compound eyes is a window into an infinite mirror corridor.
