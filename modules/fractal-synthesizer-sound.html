ECHO

Feedback delay synced to the tempo. The feedback is temporal recursion — echoes of echoes of echoes.

SHIMMER

An octave-up ghost of every note, fed back into the echo — sparkly trails that climb as they repeat.

REVERB

Convolution space with a generated impulse — a hall that was synthesized, never recorded.

FILTER

Master lowpass. Close it down for underwater depths; open it and add resonance for a singing edge.

CRUSH

Waveshaper grit on the master bus — folds the waveform over itself until it growls.

FOLD

Foldback waveshaping before the filter — instead of clipping, the waveform folds over itself, again and again. Saturation with actual folds in it.

KALEIDO DELAY

A multi-tap echo whose repeats are spaced geometrically — each tap lands ×1.618 after the last, quieter each time. The echo itself is self-similar.

MELT FREEZE

Grab the moment that is sounding right now and hold it as a smooth, crossfaded granular drone underneath everything. Press again to release it.

SHEPARD SPIRAL

A generated endless riser — a bank of octave tones gliding forever, gains shaped so the climb never arrives. A spiral staircase for pitch.

GRANULAR SHATTER

The live sound, fractaled at the millisecond level — tiny slices captured and re-scattered as grains, pitch and position shimmering.

Audio starts only when you press play. Stacked layers run through a master limiter, so the fractal can get dense without clipping harshly.
