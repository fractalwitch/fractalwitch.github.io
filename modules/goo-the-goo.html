THE MODULATIONS

  • Coils wrap your fingersHold a finger down and a double helix of goo winds around it, with rings riding up and down toward your palm. The back strands dim and the front strands glow, so it reads as wrapped around the finger rather than drawn beside it. Every haptic pulse squeezes the rings.
  • HypercubesAfter half a second, a rotating tesseract opens around the finger: its inner cube hides under your fingertip and the outer cube turns around it. Hold two fingers still and one hypercube stretches between them, inner cube on the first, outer on the second.
  • Goo stringsTwo or more fingers are joined by strands of goo. Pull them apart to stretch: the strand thins, hums at a rising pitch and the vibration tightens, until it snaps. Swipe a third finger across a strand to pluck it.
  • Hyper mirrorThree or more fingers make a mirror room: everything outside the shape your fingers make is folded back across its edges, creature included.
  • Haptic streamVibration is rescheduled every 200 ms from what the goo is doing: a purr that quickens the longer you hold, random tickle bursts, a tightening buzz as strings stretch, and a heartbeat when two fingers rest close together. Android plays the full stream. iPhone can only tick during touch events, so it ticks along as you move; turn on speaker rumble for a felt buzz through the speaker.
  • Singing and ticklingEach finger sings: height sets the note on the chosen scale, speed brightens the vowel, holding swells it. Wiggle a finger in small fast circles or back-and-forth to tickle: the goo shivers, chirps, vibrato widens, and if the creature is pressing that finger it giggles.
  • It touches backA GOOAE creature lives behind the glass. It reaches a fingertip toward every finger and presses flat against yours, ridges and all. Left alone, it taps the glass from its side: boop the tip, or press three fingers onto its hand for a high five.
  • Hand scanRest three or more fingers. It fits a circle through your fingertips to find your palm and reach, scans, then presses its own palm back in a color tuned to your hand.
  • Shape fractalsDraw a star, spiral, heart, circle, triangle, square, zigzag or ∞ in one stroke. It blooms copies of itself at golden-ratio scales, inward and outward, each with its own physics. Draw a shape inside a living one and tiny copies sprout along every layer of the outer shape. The creature traces your shape back, mirrored.
  • Hold widensA finger's goo grows the longer it stays down, and so does the spread of the paint it leaves.
  • Stillness chargeWhen nothing moves (fingers, phone, breath) a pearl charge builds. A flick spends it all in one burst.
  • Hold tunes tiltHolding a finger down makes tilt up to three times stronger, so gravity is something you grip.
  • Rhythm sets viscosityTap a steady beat: fast tempos make the goo runny, slow ones make it thick.
  • EchoThe field replays your last few seconds of gesture as a ghost, a few seconds late.
  • Two hands, two rolesWith two fingers down, the first one becomes an anchor the paint orbits. Lift it and everything collapses into it.
  • SensitizeHold perfectly still and the noise floor drops: hand tremor (from the motion sensor, or your finger's sub-pixel drift) blooms into ripples.
