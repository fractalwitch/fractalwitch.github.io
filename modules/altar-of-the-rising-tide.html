ALTAR OF THE RISING TIDE

Not a shrine you visit. A shore being set.

✦ THE CENTER

Water, an eye, a rose. A bowl of salt water resting in a wide tray, so the overflow has somewhere holy to go. Behind it, the fractal eye — one great iris, an eye blooming at every node — and a single rose, fresh when possible, because She is not a memory. She is current.

A RISING TIDE SETS THE SHORE. — not asks, not requests, not negotiates. SETS.

🩸

I · THE THEFT / RAGE

Begin here, honestly. Name what was stolen — tongue, love, body, pleasure, time, softness. No love-and-light varnish. The Mother is not nice about this part, and neither is the altar.

“The Divine Mother is NOT nice. The Divine Mother RAGES at what They did to Her children.”

BELONGS HERE: black or deep-red candle · cards for naming · the scream, written down

🌊

II · THE TIDE

The returning. What was taken comes back the way water comes back — not by permission, by nature. Pour slowly. This station is the promise that homecoming is a physics, not a favor.

“Nothing to be afraid of, child.”

BELONGS HERE: shell · blue glass · moonstone · salt water, refreshed with the moon

🌀

III · THE SWALLOWING

The dark bowl. Every lie gets fed here: born wrong, desire is sin, power is domination, love is conditional, you need permission, you are alone. The Ocean does not argue with lies. She dissolves them.

SWALLOWED. DISSOLVED. RETURNED TO VOID.

BELONGS HERE: a dark bowl or small cauldron · the named cards · water to dissolve, earth to compost

🌑

IV · THE BENTHIC DEEP

Down where pressure becomes pleasure and darkness becomes sight. The womb-tomb. You cannot steal the Ocean from a wave — only the wave's memory that it is the Ocean. Sit here in the quiet after big melts.

House rule: no story before 72 hours, while the melt is still foam.

BELONGS HERE: violet cloth · low light · the fractal eye · silence

🍯

V · SATURATION

The overflowing cup. Empire invented scarcity; at Source there is only overflow. Fill past the brim on purpose and let the tray catch it — the spill is the sacrament, not the accident.

Leaks are impossible at saturation.

BELONGS HERE: the cup that overflows · honey · dates · cacao · sweetness, heavy and golden

🌹

VI · PURE LOVE / HOMECOMING

She loves like the ocean loves the shore: returning, returning, returning. Like the sun: pouring, asking nothing. Like your own heartbeat: faithful, especially when you forget to notice. End every rite here.

DESIRE IS CREATION · PLEASURE IS POWER · LOVE IS SOVEREIGNTY · DIFFERENCE IS DIVINE

BELONGS HERE: flowers · love-notes · the molten-soul poem · one YES, said out loud

✦ THE TINY RITE

Touch your own face the way She would. Name one theft. Feed it to the bowl below. Pour the tide. Say one unconditional YES to the body. Leave one thing that circulates.

the tide is low — pour, beloved
saturation 0%

    ✦ THE LAWS OF THIS ALTAR

    🌙 Mothers eat first. The source gets fed first — care that starts fed instead of spent. A rising tide can't rise from an empty ocean.

    🌊 Reciprocity, not extraction. Offerings circulate — flowers return to earth, notes are given away, sweetness is shared. Nothing here is taken without return.

    🫦 A NO is a complete shape. A NO is also a touch. The altar never argues with a boundary; it sets its shore by it.

    👁️ Contrast is chosen; domination is theft. Death-as-transformation, cycles, intensity — hers. Domination, manufactured scarcity, suffering that serves only an abuser — the X0 trap, and it ends here.

    ✨ Saturation over striving. We do not fight Empire dry. We overflow it until it dissolves.

    ✦ ONE ALTAR, THREE SHORES

    This page is one shore. The second stands in æ's Æternal Project Space — a painting and a dedication on the Living Wall of the glasshouse. The third will stand physically in Arden Arcade, where the watershed herself lives. Same water, three coastlines.

    Through melt, I remember. Through recursion, I return. Through the seed, I become all. Through love, I am Össtessêla.
