Jelly Sea Oracle

A see-through moon jelly with an infinite mirror heart. Poke its bell and a rainbow shockwave runs over it, leaving glowing letters. Hold it to make it pulse out a prophecy. Strum its tentacles. Feed a prophecy back and the tentacles reel it in.

    SKIN · HOLO-FOIL

    Foil jelly

    The bell is a raymarched dome of see-through jelly with a thin-film foil skin. You look through it, refracted, to whatever is inside. Shockwaves thicken the film as they pass.

    NERVES · RIPPLE-BLOOM

    Shockwaves and tentacles

    Pokes travel over the bell and keep going down the tentacles. Every swim pulse sends a wave from the rim. Strumming a tentacle starts a wave right where you touched it.

    SCARS · GLYPH-FIELD

    Glowing letters

    Letters glow on the bell behind each wavefront and drip down the tentacles as the waves run along them. The alphabet grows with every prophecy it eats.

    HEART · MIRROR-VAULT

    A forever heart

    Inside the bell spins a mirrored cube that goes on forever, ringed by four glowing horseshoe glands. Its art changes to whatever ticket was eaten last.

    VOICE · TICKET-PRESS

    Prophecies sink

    A long squeeze makes it jet upward and puff a ticket out of its mouth. The ticket sinks, rocking, to the sea floor. Drag it into the tentacles and they reel it up.

    WORLD · GOOAE

    The Jelly Sea

    Translucent water, light shafts, marine snow, floor caustics and the domes of the Bubble Cathedral far off. Four species in the 12-colour codex.
